📱 Health App Sync

Web browsers can't read Samsung/Apple Health directly — those features are locked to native apps. Options:

🥗 Samsung Health (Android)
1. Open Samsung Health app
2. Tap Menu (⋮) → Settings → Download personal data
3. Or use Steps daily → tap share/export to CSV
4. Paste the CSV in the box above
🍎 Apple Health (iPhone)
1. Open Health app
2. Tap your Profile icon (top right)
3. Scroll down → Export Health Data
4. Choose CSV (you'll need to convert from the XML).
Many free converters at healthdata.org.
5. Paste steps only into the box above.
🚀 Want native auto-sync?
Wrap this app as a real iOS/Android app using Capacitor.
